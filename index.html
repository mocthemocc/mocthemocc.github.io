Projects

Energy model of an indoor ice hockey stadium

Curtin University · group project

An ice hockey stadium has to be heated and refrigerated at the same time: the upper zone is kept at a comfortable temperature for spectators, while the lower zone sits directly above the ice. We model the building as these two lumped thermal zones.

  • Steady-state energy model covering heat transfer between the zones, to the ice, to the non-ice floor and through the building envelope
  • Analytical solutions for the zone temperatures and the heating and cooling power required
  • Energy consumption estimates validated against published measurements

Human vs AI portfolio management

Curtin University · group project

We managed a simulated AUD 300,000 equity portfolio on StockTrak alongside a second AUD 300,000 portfolio that followed generative-AI recommendations without modification, then compared the two.

  • Risk-adjusted performance: Sharpe and Sortino ratios, Jensen's alpha and the impact of exchange rates
  • Market-wide (systematic) and stock-specific (idiosyncratic) drivers of performance
  • What the results say about market efficiency, including behavioural biases
